Our mission is to apply engineering principles to the design and construction of bicycles, giving students real-world experience through collaborative, project-based learning.

Our Ride or Die.

Founded in Fall 2021, we are a student engineering club pioneering the bike building field among universities. We design, build, test, and ride bikes, fabricating the frame from scratch and even making our own composites.

We have open enrollment and encourage all majors and people to join our wonderful community! There is every type of work imaginable, from business to fabrication.

We design, build, and test all things bikes with a focus on frames from scratch.

We design, build, test, and ride bikes! Many people think this means we put components on a frame. More than that, we design and fabricate the frame from scratch. We even make our own composites. Our club was founded Fall 2021 by Ziven Posner and Jacob Pashman. It has been a long ride, but luckily some of us are randonneurs :)

The club is open to all majors and we welcome everyone. We look for members with a strong work ethic and good communication. An interest in cycling is certainly a plus.

Competition.

Every spring we take our latest build to the Sea Otter Classic in Monterey, California, one of the biggest cycling festivals in North America. Alongside the races and the industry expo, Sea Otter hosts an intercollegiate bike building competition where student teams from schools like Cal Poly, UC Irvine, the University of Victoria, and Penn State show off frames and components they designed and built themselves.
