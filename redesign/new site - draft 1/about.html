Our mission is to apply engineering principles to the design and construction of bicycles, giving students real-world experience through collaborative, project-based learning.

Our Ride or Die.

Founded in Fall 2021, we are a new engineering club pioneering the bike building field among universities. We design, build, test, and ride bikes, fabricating the frame from scratch and even make our own custom components.

We have open enrollment and encourage all majors and people to join our wonderful community! There is every type of work imaginable, from business to FEA.

We design, build, and test all things bikes with a focus on frames from scratch.

We design, build, test, and ride bikes! Many people think this means we put components on a frame. More than that, we design and fabricate the frame from scratch. We even make some custom components. Our club was founded Fall 2021 by Ziven Posner and Jacob Pashman. It has been a long ride — luckily some of us are randonneurs :)

All members are current UC Berkeley students. The club is open to all majors and we welcome everyone. We look for members with a strong work ethic and good communication. An interest in cycling is certainly a plus.
