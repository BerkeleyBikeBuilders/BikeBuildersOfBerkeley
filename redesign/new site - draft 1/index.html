Who are we?

Founded in Fall 2021, we are a new engineering club pioneering the bike building field among universities. We design, build, test, and ride bikes, fabricating the frame from scratch and even make our own custom components.

We have open enrollment and encourage all majors and people to join our wonderful community! There is every type of work imaginable, from business to FEA.

We design, build, and test all things bikes with a focus on frames from scratch.

Learn More

Designing.

Our club focuses on the full design process behind high-performance bicycles, from early concept sketches to detailed CAD models.

Members collaborate to explore geometry, materials, and ergonomics, learning how thoughtful design choices directly impact performance, safety, and rider experience.

Our Designs

Engineering.

We apply engineering principles to turn designs into real, rideable systems.

Through analysis, prototyping, and testing, members gain hands-on experience with structural mechanics, manufacturing methods, and system integration — bridging classroom theory with real-world engineering challenges.

Our Gears

Learning.

Above all, we are a learning-driven community.

Whether you’re new to engineering or bringing years of experience, the club provides opportunities to develop technical skills, ask questions, and learn from peers. We emphasize mentorship, experimentation, and growth at every stage.

Our Work.

Past projects showcase our commitment to building, testing, and refining real systems. By working on complete bicycle platforms and subsystems, members gain hands-on experience transforming ideas into functional, rideable outcomes.

Our past projects highlight a student-driven approach to engineering, where curiosity and iteration lead the way. From early prototypes to finished builds, we take pride in creating thoughtful, well-engineered solutions that reflect both technical rigor and creativity.
